Team Trends

Every tracked team's own rolling-10-game average, across all markets. This is the raw material the matchup projections above are built from.

Methodology. Each matchup projection blends one team's own rolling-10-game average with the specific opponent's own rolling-10-game average at allowing/conceding that stat — e.g. a corners projection averages Team A's own corners-won rate with Team B's own corners-conceded rate, so it's a read on this specific pairing, not a comparison to the league at large. The headline number is that projection minus the standard betting line for the market (shown in brackets), so +2.1 means we project 2.1 above the typical line. Hit-rate is the share of the team's last 10 games that landed over its own individual line. Match cards sums both teams' own card rates directly, compared against a 3.5 total-cards line. Newly promoted teams with fewer than 6 top-flight games are topped up with their most recent Championship form, flagged inline. Data: football-data.co.uk. This page surfaces statistical trends only — it is not betting advice, and past trends are not a guarantee of what happens next.
